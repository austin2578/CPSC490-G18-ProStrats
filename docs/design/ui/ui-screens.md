# UI screens

> Task: #76 · Owner: Max Armenta · Sprint: 3 · Status: draft

This document shows the prototype UI screens for Project Rogue and explains what each one is for.

## Screens

### 1. Title screen
![Title screen](ui/Project Rouge.png)

The first screen the player sees. It has the game name and three buttons: Start game, Settings, and Quit.

### 2. Class selection
![Class selection](ui/Class Selection.png)

The player picks a starting class and playstyle from three cards. Each card shows a portrait, class name, playstyle summary, starting weapon, traversal utility, and focus attributes. Back and Confirm buttons sit below.

### 3. Route selection
![Route selection](ui/Route Selection.png)

The player chooses the next path through the run. A map at the top shows cleared rooms, the current position, and the paths ahead. Three path cards show the room type, a reward preview, and the difficulty.

### 4. Reward selection
![Reward selection](ui/Reward Selection.png)

Shown after an encounter is cleared. The player picks one of three rewards (weapon, boon, or skill) or skips the reward.

### 5. Gameplay HUD
![Gameplay HUD](ui/Gameplay Hub.png)

The in-game display. Health, stamina, and mana bars sit top-left, the encounter tracker top-right, the equipped weapon and its mastery bottom-left, and the three skill slots (keys 1 to 3) bottom-center. Enemies have health bars above their heads.

### 6. Inventory and equipment
![Inventory and equipment](ui/Invetory and Equipment.png)

Shows the active weapon with its base damage, attribute scaling, and affixes, plus the three equipped skills with their costs and cooldowns.

### 7. Character and build
![Character and build](ui/Charcter Build.png)

Shows the character's attributes, the boons collected so far, and any active status effects.

### 8. Pause menu
![Pause menu](ui/Pause Menu.png)

Opens during play and freezes the game. Buttons: Resume, Controls, Settings, and Quit to menu.

### 9. Settings
![Settings](ui/Settings Menu.png)

Audio sliders for master volume, music, and effects, plus the list of controls, with Back and Apply buttons.

### 10. Death
![Death](ui/Death Menu.png)

Shown when the player's health reaches 0. It shows run statistics and offers Try again or Quit to menu.

### 11. Run complete
![Run complete](ui/Run Complete.png)

Shown when the player finishes a run. It shows the run summary and offers New run or Quit to menu.
